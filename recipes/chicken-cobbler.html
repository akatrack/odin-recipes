Chicken Cobbler

Description

Chicken pieces with mushrooms in butter, simmered with milk, water, stock, and lemon juice. Topped with a simple scone dough and brushed with milk, this chicken cobbler completes to a golden, glazed delight.

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken joints
  • 1 and 1/2 oz. butter
  • 4 oz. mushrooms
  • 1/2 pint milk
  • 1 chicken stock cube
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 9 oz. plain flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 3 oz. margarine
  • 2 sprigs parsley

Preparation time

25 minutes

Cooking time

45 minutes

Oven setting

425°F; Gas Mark 7

Steps

  1. Cut each chicken joint in half.
  2. Melt the butter and fry the chicken and mushrooms for 5 minutes.
  3. Remove the mushrooms.
  4. Add the milk, water, stock cube and lemon juice to the chicken, and bring to the boil.
  5. Cover and cook for 30 minutes.
  6. Mix 1 oz. flour to a smooth paste with a little water.
  7. Remove the chicken, cut off the flesh and return to the liquid with the flour paste.
  8. Bring to the boil, stirring until thickened.
  9. Add the mushrooms.
  10. Make the scone topping.
  11. Sift the flour and baking powder together and add the margarine.
  12. Cut into small pieces and rub in until the mixture resembles fine breadcrumbs.
  13. Add sufficient water to make a soft dough.
  14. Press out on a floured surface to 1/2 inch thickness, and shape to size of dish.
  15. Turn the chicken mixture into an ovenproof dish.
  16. Place the scone mixture on top and mark into a criss-cross pattern.
  17. Brush with milk and bake in the centre of the oven for 10 minutes or until well risen and golden brown.
  18. Serve garnished with parsley.
Serves 4.
