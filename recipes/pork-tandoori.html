Pork Tandoori

You will need...

  • 4 pork chops
  • 8 oz. Patna rice
  • 1/4 teaspoon powdered saffron
      • For the marinade:
    • 2 5 oz. cartons neutral yogurt
    • 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
    • 3/4 tablespoon paprika pepper
    • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder or
    • 1 clove garlic, finely crushed
    • 4 bay leaves
    • 6 peppercorns
    • 1 tablespoon tomato puree
    • grated zest of 1 lemon
    • 1 teaspoon salt

Preparation time

15 minutes plus 6-8 hours to marinate

Cooking time

1 hour 15 minutes

Oven Setting

375°F; Gas Mark 5

Instructions

Prick pork chops well with a fork or skewer and place in a casserole dish. Place ingredients for marinade in a bowl or jug and mix well. Pour over pork making sure that they are all covered with the marinade.

Cover dish with foil or lid and leave for 6-8 hours. At the end of this time remove bay leaves and peppercorn. Remove lid from casserole dish and baste pork. Re-cover and bake in a moderate oven for about 1 and 1/4 hours, basting occasionally until all the marinade in used. 15-20 minutes before the end of cooking time, cook rice in boiling salted water with the saffron.

Drain rice and place on a round serving dish. Top with the chops and serve. Peas can be added to the rice if liked.

Serves 4.
